Zuppa Toscana

Classic soup from Olive Garden with Italian sausage, bacon, kale, and potatoes in a rich creamy broth.

Ingredients

  • 1lbs ground Italian sausage
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons red pepper flakes
  • 1 large white onion
  • 4 stripes bacon, diced
  • 2 teaspoons garlic puree
  • 10 cups water
  • 5 cubes chicken bouillon
  • 1lbs russet potato, diced
  • 1 cup Heavy whipping cream
  • 1/4 bunch kale, chopped

Directions

  1. Dice bacon stripes into bite size pieces. Remove once cooked.
  2. Combine sausage and red papper flakes. Remove once cooked.
  3. Add diced white onions into pot, leave oil/fat from bacon and sausage in pot to cook onions until onions become translucent. Then add garlic and cook for about 30 seconds. Do not over cook garlic or it will be bitter.
  4. Add water and chicken bouillon cubes into pot. While the water boils, cut the potatoes into bite sized cubes.
  5. Once water boils add potatoes for around 10-15 minutes or until potatoes are fork tender.
  6. Return cooked bacon and sausage back into pot.
  7. Add heavy whipping cream and stir until combined. Finish with chopped kale.
